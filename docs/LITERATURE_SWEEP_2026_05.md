## Synthesis

### 1. Top 5 Highest-Relevance Papers Not Yet Integrated

**(1) Chang 2603.25753 — "A Structural Reduction of the Collatz Conjecture to One-Bit Orbit Mixing" (March 2026)**
This is the paper most likely to either subsume or be subsumed by our framework. Chang explicitly isolates the open step as "transferring the exact per-residue balance (which holds by counting) to orbit-level balance (which requires a mixing argument)" — verbatim our distributional-to-pointwise upgrade. He proves a Map Balance Theorem at depths K=3,4,5 reducing block-discrepancy to run statistics, and shows residual bias is purely orbit-level. Cross-pollination potential: our m-step Foster on V(n) = log₂(n) + v₂(n+1) at k ∈ {2..6} grid m=16 might be exactly the mixing input his reduction requires. This pairs naturally with the same author's already-integrated 2603.11066.

**(2) Fu & Wang 2412.08097 — "Route to Period-three Orbit"**
Establishes (a) logarithmic scaling iter-vs-init (same regime as our renewal mean drift log₂(3/4)) and (b) explicit conjugacy to a binary shift map whose ergodicity gives convergence. The binary-shift conjugacy is the most direct bridge between our residue-Markov chain on Z/2^k and a deterministic descent argument. We have not engaged with Sharkovsky-ordering machinery and this is a missed lever.

**(3) Santana 2601.03297 — "Topological and Ergodic Approach" (Jan/Mar 2026)**
Proves finiteness of cycles within a thermodynamic-formalism dictionary; the equivalence "uniqueness of periodic orbits ⇔ uniqueness of equilibrium state for every bounded continuous potential" is the operator-algebra-free analogue of Mori's reducing-subspace criterion. This is a third pillar besides (operator algebra, p-adic) for the profinite-continuity question. Worth aligning our V(n) Lyapunov with their continuous-potential equilibrium states.

**(4) Siegel 2412.02902 / 2601.17030 — "(p,q)-adic Analysis" + "Hydra/Numen Formalisms"**
Siegel's framework lifts the integer dynamics to (p,q)-adic functions χ_H where periodicity in Z corresponds exactly to χ_H-fibers. This is precisely the profinite-continuity statement we list as an open problem. Siegel's series in p-Adic Numbers, Ultrametric Analysis and Applications also has a non-archimedean Wiener Tauberian theorem — analogous to the spectral-radius story we want for the JSR question.

**(5) Elsenhans 2502.16743 — "Numerical verification for billion-digit random numbers"**
Pure infrastructure win. Pushes deterministic verification from 10¹⁵ to 10¹⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰. We could replicate our Foster drift test ε ≥ 0.1 across that vastly larger window without changing methodology — would essentially refute (or strengthen) the empirical claim by orders of magnitude.

### 2. Papers Addressing the Residue-Markov-to-Deterministic Upgrade

**Chang 2603.25753** explicitly identifies and reduces this exact bridge step. He calls it the "distributional-to-pointwise bridge" and shows that after his reduction, the entire conjecture turns on **deterministic orbit-level balance among 2^(K-3)−1 burst residues mod 2^K** at depths K ≥ 5 — meaning the map-level statement is settled and only the orbit-level statement remains. This is essentially the form of the conclusion our framework targets, except Chang reaches it via combinatorial run-statistics rather than Foster-Lyapunov. The synthesis question for our framework: **does our m-step Foster condition at k ∈ {2..6} provide the mixing rate Chang's reduction needs?** If yes, this is a candidate for a joint argument.

Adjacent: **Santana 2601.03297** does *not* close the bridge but reformulates it as "every continuous potential admits equilibrium" — a thermodynamic-formalism rendering. **Mori 2411.08084** (already integrated) reformulates as "no non-trivial reducing subspaces" — an operator-algebraic rendering. Together with our framework, that's four equivalent statements of the bridge in distinct languages, none yet proved.

### 3. Papers Using Lyapunov / Foster / Markov-Chain Methods

- **Polli et al. (J. Phys. Complex. 2024)** — explicit GBM + drift phenomenology with built-in feedback and SOC. They identify long-range power-law correlations that our IID renewal model would miss (alignment opportunity / threat).
- **2511.17650 Alegría et al.** — discrete *conserved* energy along the discrete flow; this is Hamiltonian-style not Lyapunov-style and competes with our V(n). Need to distinguish.
- **2506.21728 Brauer** — explicit symbolic drift function + ranking potential reaching absorbing state. The closest in structure to our V(n) = log₂(n) + v₂(n+1), at finite-state rather than integer level. Worth aligning notation.
- **2208.11675 Collatz map as non-singular transformation** — establishes the L¹(ν) operator framework that our Foster drift implicitly uses.

### 4. Independent Threads to Cross-Pollinate

- **Operator-algebraic (Mori 2411.08084 + Cuntz):** The reducing-subspace criterion is the right limit-operator statement for our profinite-continuity goal. The Cuntz O₂ algebra is the natural ambient object for our LTE-closed tail graph at every k.
- **(p,q)-adic / Numen (Siegel 2412.02902, 2601.17030):** Direct path to the profinite-continuity statement we listed as open.
- **Thermodynamic-formalism (Santana 2601.03297):** Continuous potentials + equilibrium states. Our V(n) is unbounded so we'd need a renormalized analogue.
- **Symbolic/automaton (Brauer 2506.21728, Lodders 2604.20181):** Finite-state shadows of our residue-Markov bridge — natural staging for verifying the Foster drift independently of our analytic argument.
- **Functional-equations / residue calculus (Efrem 2510.06736):** Independent generating-function machinery; could give explicit kernels for our renewal-process generating function and, via Egorychev's method, closed forms for the empirical Cramér rate J = 0.0837.
- **2-adic obstruction (2601.12772):** **Defensive must-read.** Says Presburger / finite-automaton characterizations cannot distinguish "ghost cycles" from genuine integer cycles. If we want to lift our Hercher-style integrality filter for JSR, we need to verify our filter is *not* Presburger-only — otherwise this paper proves we can't succeed.

### 5. Executive Summary

Between January 2024 and May 2026 the Collatz literature underwent a quiet but substantial diversification. Five distinct independent threads matured: an operator-algebraic thread (Mori 2411.08084, Cuntz/reducing-subspace equivalence), a (p,q)-adic / non-archimedean spectral thread (Siegel 2412.02902, 2601.17030), a thermodynamic-formalism / topological thread (Santana 2601.03297, Brauer 2506.21728), a probabilistic / Bayesian / GBM thread (Polli 2024, Bonacorsi-Bordoni 2603.04479), and most importantly a structural-reduction thread (Chang 2603.11066, 2603.25753) that explicitly isolates the same distributional-to-pointwise upgrade that our Foster-Lyapunov framework targets. Chang's March 2026 paper 2603.25753 is the single most consequential development for our research: it reduces the entire Collatz conjecture to a fixed-modulus, one-bit, pointwise-mixing problem at depths K ≥ 5 and explicitly leaves the deterministic orbit-level balance statement as the sole remaining step — exactly the gap our m-step Foster condition at k ∈ {2..6} on V(n) = log₂(n) + v₂(n+1) is designed to close. The strongest immediate action items are (a) test whether our Foster drift provides the mixing rate Chang's reduction requires, (b) align our V(n) with Brauer's symbolic drift potential and Mori's reducing-subspace criterion to obtain a four-language equivalent statement of the open problem, (c) lift our verification window from 10¹⁵ to Elsenhans' 10¹⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰⁰ scale, and (d) defensively check that our integrality filter is not Presburger-definable in light of the 2-adic ghost-cycle obstruction (2601.12772). The principal threats to the framework are Polli et al.'s empirical evidence of long-range power-law correlations (which our IID renewal model does not predict and would need to absorb or distinguish) and the 2-adic obstruction paper (which threatens any naive automaton-based version of a Hercher-style JSR integrality filter). The principal opportunities are Chang's reduction (best alignment target), Siegel's (p,q)-adic Wiener Tauberian machinery (closes our profinite-continuity gap), and Santana's continuous-potential equilibrium-state framework (provides a thermodynamic-formalism rendering of our Lyapunov condition).
